# Contact Cognify & Initialtide

> Reach the Cognify maintainers for curriculum corrections, exam-board syllabus requests, accessibility reports, API or MCP integration questions, school support, or privacy inquiries.

## Support Hours & Response SLA

- **Hours:** Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 GMT/BST (GitHub Issues monitored daily)
- **Response Time:** Within 2 business days for email or GitHub issues

## How to Report Curriculum or Technical Issues

1. **Curriculum or Mark-Scheme Corrections:** Include the qualification (GCSE, IGCSE, or A-Level), exam board, course ID (for example `gcse-biology-aqa-higher`), and subtopic title.
2. **Bug Reports & Accessibility Feedback:** Open a public issue at [https://github.com/initialtide/Cognify/issues](https://github.com/initialtide/Cognify/issues) or email support@cognify.initialtide.org with your browser and theme (Light or Dark).
3. **Developer, API & MCP Inquiries:** Review [https://initialtide.github.io/Cognify/docs](https://initialtide.github.io/Cognify/docs) and [https://initialtide.github.io/Cognify/mcp](https://initialtide.github.io/Cognify/mcp) first, then contact us for additional curriculum fields or schema extensions.
