Cognify — Free GCSE, IGCSE & A-Level Interactive Revision Platform

Cognify is an open-access GCSE revision, IGCSE revision, and A-Level revision platform built by Initialtide. Study 47 structured courses and 617+ subtopics across AQA, Edexcel, OCR, and Cambridge International (CIE) with interactive video lessons, structured revision notes, multi-format quizzes, flashcards, exam-style practice, and official Cambridge past papers — completely free with no paywalls.
